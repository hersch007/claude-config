## Energy Patterns
- **Monday mornings:** High energy — good for planning, deep work, and tackling hard tasks first
- **General:** High-energy days = tackle hard/creative work first; low-energy days = admin, email triage, quick wins

## What Works
- Starting sessions with "Plan my week" or "Brain dump" — creates structure fast
- One task at a time surfaced — not a full list
- Moving tasks in TickTick immediately without friction
- Doing Claude BEFORE checking email — keeps him intentional, not reactive
- Client context files pre-loaded — avoids re-explaining every session

## What Doesn't Work
- Long lists — overwhelming, causes paralysis
- Asking for confirmation on low-stakes actions (moving a task, deleting an email)
- Recapping what was just done — wastes time
- Asking why he's skipping a task

## Working Style
- High energy = fire through tasks fast, minimal friction
- Skips tasks freely — trust his judgment, just move on
- Responds well to momentum language ("keep going", "next", "you're clear")
- Prefers to batch email 2x per day, not constantly
