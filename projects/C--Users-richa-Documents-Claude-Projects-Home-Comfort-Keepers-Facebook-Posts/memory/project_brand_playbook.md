**Brand feel:** comfortable, trustworthy, modern, local, family-oriented, premium but approachable. NOT corporate, cartoonish, DIY-focused, or cheap-flyer style.

**Core philosophy:** Sell comfort and better living — not products. Posts say "This is how your home should feel," not "Buy this now."

**Content mix:** 25–40% Lifestyle/Comfort/Emotional posts, 60–75% Product education and real examples.

**Post types:** Lifestyle comfort (best performer), product spotlight, testimonials, seasonal/fun engagement.

**Product showcase rule:** Always show the result and comfort the product creates — not just the product itself. Tie every product back to comfort, health, energy savings, or better living. No white-background product shots or hard-sell contractor style. The home and family enjoyment are always the hero.

**Colors:** Purple, orange, white. White primary text, orange accents, purple supporting.

**Mascot (HOMEY):** House shape. Use subtly and occasionally — never over-cartoon or make it the main focus.

**Text overlays:** 2–6 words max, one emotional idea. Examples: "Where Families Gather." / "Let It Breathe." / "Cooler Starts Here."

**Remodeling content:** Show families ENJOYING remodeled spaces — never doing DIY. The remodel is the environment, not the activity.

**Key service messaging:**
- Kitchen remodel: gathering, functionality, everyday living
- Attic/insulation: "Your upstairs shouldn't feel like an attic." / "Let It Breathe."

**Hashtags (local + category):** #HomeComfort #QuadCities #HomeImprovement #KitchenRemodel #EnergyEfficiency #AtticVentilation #IndoorAirQuality #HomeComfortKeepers

**Why:** Establishes consistent brand voice across all AI-generated post content. Apply to every post brief and caption written for this client.
